Financial vendor invoices contain sensitive banking, GSTIN, and commercial terms. Running local models via Ollama or Nous Hermes ensures zero customer or accounting data leaves company firewalls, fulfilling GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC-2 requirements.

Self-Correction Reasoning

When an invoice contains messy OCR noise or arithmetic rounding mismatches, an autonomous agent inspects the validator's feedback, reasons through the error, and autonomously repairs table alignments before outputting final records.
